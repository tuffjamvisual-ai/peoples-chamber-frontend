## Verified against external sources (all exact — no trims required)
- **British Steel**: Parliament recalled on a Saturday (12 Apr 2025), emergency
  Act passed in one day giving Reynolds power to direct the company and secure
  raw materials; ~3,500 jobs; Britain's last primary (blast-furnace) steelmaking
  at Scunthorpe. £484m of working capital by May 2026. NAO: no repayment
  schedule, unlikely to be repaid, ~£1.3m/day, no set end date. All confirmed.
- **India trade deal**: signed July 2025 by Reynolds and Piyush Goyal; +£4.8bn
  to GDP annually and +£25.5bn bilateral trade — both explicitly **2040
  forecasts** (the bio frames them correctly as forecasts). Talks predated
  Labour. Confirmed.
- **US deal**: quota for British vehicle exports at lower tariff; steel-tariff
  relief delayed/conditional — consistent with the May 2025 US-UK arrangement.
- **Solicitor / SRA**: trained ~10 months at Addleshaw Goddard, never qualified;
  repeatedly called himself a solicitor; SRA closed with no further action but
  warned that repeating the claim could risk prosecution. Confirmed.
- Career arc (2016 resignation over McFadden's sacking, backed Owen Smith,
  shadow City minister, Business & Trade Secretary 2024, Chief Whip Sep 2025,
  restored the whip to four welfare rebels): consistent with the record.
