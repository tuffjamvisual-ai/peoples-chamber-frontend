## Correction — Victims and Courts Act 2026 (overstated her role)
- Draft claimed the government "accepted the principle of her amendment" and that the
  "resulting law" covers "an offence against a child and has parental responsibility
  for **another** child" — i.e. it credited her (unadopted) amendment as enacted.
- Reality: the **Act** (Royal Assent **29 April 2026**) imposes a prohibited steps
  order where a **4+ year** sentence is given for a serious sexual offence against a
  child **for whom the offender has parental responsibility** (or a rape-conceived
  child) — the offender's OWN child. **Asato's Amendment 9** (any child / other
  children / sentences under 4 years) "has not been considered by the House" — not
  adopted in full.
- BUT the MoJ press release ("Thousands of children protected…", 21 Oct 2025) credits
  the reform to the campaigns of **Natalie Fleet, Baroness Harman and Jess Asato**.
- Rewrote the section to: state the Act accurately; describe Asato's amendment as a bid
  to widen it; note the government credited her campaigning; and say her wider amendment
  was not adopted in full. Removed "accepted the principle of her amendment" and
  "materially widened an Act of Parliament".
