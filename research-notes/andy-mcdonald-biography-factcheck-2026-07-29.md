## Current role (per standing "always check the role is current" rule)
- **Backbench Labour MP** for Middlesbrough and Thornaby East (MP since 2012).
  No frontbench/government post since resigning as shadow employment rights
  secretary in September 2021. Bio correctly frames him as a backbench

## Verified (kept)
- Rail spokesman Jan 2016; shadow transport secretary from 27 June 2016; held
  the brief ~4 years (to 2020). Confirmed.
- 2019 nominated Rebecca Long-Bailey; retained by Starmer as shadow employment
  rights secretary; resigned Sept 2021 over instruction to argue against a £15
  minimum wage and living-wage sick pay ("This is something I could not do").
  Confirmed / well documented.
- 2025 disability-benefit rebellion (120+ MPs, PIP concessions) — consistent
  with the rest of this bio series. Kept.
- **Passenger Railway Services (Public Ownership) Act 2024 — Royal Assent 28
  November 2024.** Confirmed exactly.
- **Employment Rights Act 2025** — removed the SSP waiting period and lower
  earnings limit, repealed the majority of the Trade Union Act 2016,
  established the **Fair Work Agency** (came into being 7 April 2026).
  Confirmed. Rayner-led. Kept.
- **Minimum wage £12.71** (National Living Wage, 21+, from 1 April 2026, up 4.1
  per cent from £12.21). Confirmed — the "not £15" point holds.
- **Teesworks review** — found excessive confidentiality/lack of transparency,
  weak oversight, but **no evidence of corruption or illegality**; McDonald had
  alleged "industrial-scale corruption." Confirmed.
- **River-to-the-sea suspension** — suspended Oct 2023; **whip restored 13
  March 2024** after an internal investigation found no rule breach (with a
  warning about contentious language). Confirmed (ITV, 13 March 2024).

## Corrected / trimmed before publication (Burnham rule)
- **Rail public-ownership timing** — bio said "by June 2026 most passenger
  services had returned to public ownership" (stated twice). This is WRONG:
  transfers began in 2025 as franchise contracts expired (South Western May
  2025, c2c July 2025, Greater Anglia autumn 2025) and all franchises are not
  due to expire until October 2027. Rewritten to: the Act "made a publicly
  owned operator the default, and services began returning to public ownership
  from 2025 as franchise contracts expired, a process due to complete by 2027."
  Dropped the duplicate/incorrect "government reported most services publicly
  owned by June 2026" closing sentence.
- **Stripped trailing citation sentences** (source tags in sentence form):
  "Parliament records the dates of his appointments"; "Long-Bailey's
  parliamentary nominations included McDonald"; "His resignation letter set out
  the instruction and his reasons"; "Hansard records McDonald urging MPs to
  reject the bill" (kept the content "McDonald urged MPs to reject the bill");
  "The green paper identifies his role..."; "The government's implementation
  record distinguishes..."; "The official 2026 minimum-wage rate was £12.71"
  (duplicate); "The review records both findings"; "ITV reported the finding
  and complete outcome."
