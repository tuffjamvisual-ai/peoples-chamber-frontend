## Verified against external sources (all confirmed — no trims required)
- **P&O Ferries (Oct 2024)**: called P&O a "rogue operator" and urged a boycott
  over the 2022 sacking of 800 seafarers; DP World (parent) paused a planned
  £1bn investment and skipped the investment summit; Starmer distanced the
  government ("not the view of the government") before the investment was
  rescued. Confirmed.
- **Passenger Railway Services (Public Ownership) Act 2024**: introduced within
  ~two weeks of taking office; royal assent 28 Nov 2024; public operation the
  default as contracts expire; SWR/c2c/Greater Anglia transfers happened under
  her successor. Confirmed (28 Nov also cross-checked in the Alexander note).
- **ASLEF settlement**: compounded ~15% (5% 2022-23 + 4.75% 2023-24 + 4.5%
  2024-25 = ~14.9%), backdated and pensionable, "no strings"; 18 strike days;
  ended the two-year dispute; 96% ratified. Confirmed. Bio's "almost 15 per
  cent … no working-practice or productivity reforms" is accurate.
- **Conviction/resignation**: resigned Nov 2024 (first ministerial exit under
  Starmer); 2014 guilty plea to fraud by false representation over a work phone
  reported missing after a 2013 mugging, then found; conditional discharge
  (lowest outcome); spent. Confirmed.
- Buses: extended franchising, ~£1bn support; direction later embodied in the
  Bus Services Act 2025 (passed under Heidi Alexander). Consistent with the
  Alexander note. NDA backbench campaign: qualitative, kept.
