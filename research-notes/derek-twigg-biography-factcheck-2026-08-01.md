## Verified (kept — confirmed)
- Entered 1997; whips' office under Blair; schools minister (booed 2005 over
  performance tables); **rail minister then Minister for Veterans 2006**; **Brown
  removed him Oct 2008**; shadow health 2010. Confirmed.
- **Railways for All**, launched **March 2006**, **£370m** fund (Twigg wrote the
  foreword); **Access for All** programme continued long after. Confirmed.
- **The Nation's Commitment**, **July 2008**, first cross-government service-
  welfare strategy (47 commitments); priority NHS treatment for service-
  attributable conditions (with Alan Johnson); Iraq Inquiry recorded it, noting
  limited practical value; predated the statutory Armed Forces Covenant.
  Confirmed.
- **£16.6m** Halton community diagnostics (Warrington & Halton Diagnostics Centre
  + Halton Health Hub, Runcorn); larger replacement hospital not delivered.
  Confirmed.
- Backed Burnham 2010/2015/2026, Nandy 2020; opposed same-sex marriage 2013;
  resisted assisted dying; accepted Brexit / opposed a second referendum. Kept.

## Corrected / softened / dropped

  of thousands** of additional tests and appointments a year". The 64,000 couldn't

  eliminate"** → "**billions of pounds … many years**". The 2008-era specifics
  couldn't be confirmed this session.

  A source indicates Twigg **supported the welfare cuts** in correspondence, so the
  "voted against" claim is contested/unconfirmed. Removed; the "not an automatic
  loyalist" point is carried by the Brexit example.
