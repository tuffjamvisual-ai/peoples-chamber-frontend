## Current role (per standing "always check the role is current" rule)
- **CONFIRMED current**: Burnham appointed Kinnock **Secretary of State for
  Wales** on 20 July 2026 (replacing Jo Stevens); previously Minister of State
  for Care at DHSC. Son of Neil Kinnock; MP for Aberafan Maesteg. Sources:
  Caerphilly Observer, ITV Wales, Nation.Cymru, Herald.Wales. "Nine days old by
  29 July" is consistent (appointed 20 July).

## Verified against external sources (kept)
- **NHS dental contract**: biggest change since 2006, from April 2026, embedding
  urgent care with higher payments; announced by Kinnock as Care Minister.
  Confirmed.
- **Adult Social Care Fair Pay Agreement / negotiating body**: announced 16 July
  2026 (unions + employers, independent chair from early 2027); £500m in 2028-29;
  1.5 million-worker sector; first negotiations April 2027, first settlement
  April 2028. All confirmed.
- **National Care Service / Casey**: Commission launched April 2025, full report
  due 2028; Kinnock told the Committee he disagreed with the Treasury's push to
  delay reform to 2029. Confirmed.
- **Care-worker training fund cancelled**: the Adult Social Care Training and
  Development Fund was discontinued ("a significant blow", Nuffield Trust). Kept,
  but dropped the unverified "2024" year → "during the government's spending
  reductions".
- **Port Talbot**: blast furnace closed 30 Sept 2024 (ended 100+ years of primary
  steelmaking); £500m UK-government support within Tata's £1.25bn electric-arc
  furnace (due end 2027). Confirmed. Jobs corrected: ~2,000 at Port Talbot
  (2,800 across Tata UK) — bio's "1,900" changed to "roughly 2,000".

## Trimmed / corrected before publication (Burnham rule)
- **Dental delivery "nearly one million slots commissioned … only ~100,000
  urgent appointments used … 1.8 million courses of treatment"** → Full Fact
  says **no data has been published on appointments delivered**, and DHSC's own
  costing put the 700k pledge at ~324,000 courses (not 1.8m). Reworked to: the
  700k pledge is real (ICBs to commission from April 2025), but no delivery
  figures were published, so the promise could not be shown to be met.
- **Vacancy figures "126,000 (2023/24) → 111,000 (2024/25)"** → the confirmed
  2023/24-ish figure is ~131,000 (Skills for Care), and 111,000 was unconfirmed;
  dropped the exact numbers. Kept the confirmed point: vacancies had been falling
  mainly on earlier international recruitment, not his (not-yet-effective) policy.
- **Port Talbot "roughly 1,900 direct jobs"** → "roughly 2,000" (confirmed).
