## Current role (per standing "always check the role is current" rule)
- **CONFIRMED**: MP for Stretford and Urmston since 15 Dec 2022 by-election.
  Trafford Council leader from 2018 (stepped down Jan 2023). Opposition Whip

  DWP Parliamentary Under-Secretary from 9 July 2024, titled Minister for
  Transformation — portfolio covers fraud/error/debt, digital/AI/service
  modernisation, customer experience. Promoted to **Minister of State at DWP
  under Burnham, 21 July 2026** (externally reported reshuffle → no Open Govt
  note). NB gov.uk ministers page still cached his PUS title at time of check.

## Verified (kept)
- **Public Authorities (Fraud, Error and Recovery) Act 2025** — Royal Assent /
  became law **2 December 2025** (legislation.gov.uk ukpga/2025/28). Expanded
  DWP info/investigation powers, eligibility checks via financial-institution
  data, direct recovery from bank accounts. Cabinet Office holds the wider
  public-sector fraud provisions (so "cannot claim the whole Act" holds).
- **£1.5bn** OBR-scored benefits (by 2029-30); DWP later cited **£2.1bn over
  five years** as part of a wider savings plan. Confirmed both figures exist.
- **Digital proof of entitlement** launched **September 2025 across eight
  benefit lines** (instant online proof, 24/7). Confirmed.
- **Overpayment rate 3.3 per cent in 2024-25** (FYE2025, ~£9.5bn), down from
  3.6 per cent / £9.7bn in FYE2024. Confirmed.
- **Sayce review** — Independent Review of Carer's Allowance Overpayments (Liz
  Sayce OBE): systemic failings, incorrect earnings-averaging guidance; DWP
  accepted guidance had misstated the law and updated it; ~26,000 carers to be
  put right, ~200,000 cases to review (~two years). Confirmed.
- NAO report "Tackling benefit overpayments due to fraud and error" — real;
  qualitative findings (headway on UC, still too high, weak implementation
  plan) kept as attributed NAO judgement.
