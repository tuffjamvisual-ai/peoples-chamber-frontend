# Internal research note — Dame Nia Griffith biography fact-check (2026-07-31)

Internal only. Backs the biography on her profile (`mp_biography.political_bio`,

## Current role (per standing "always check the role is current" rule)
- **CONFIRMED**: now a **backbench Labour MP** for **Llanelli** (since 2005).
  Was **PUSS Wales** (9 July 2024 – 7 Sept 2025) and **PUSS Equalities** (8 Oct
  2024 – 7 Sept 2025); **left government 7 Sept 2025** (~14 months). **Shadow
  Defence Secretary 6 Oct 2016 – 6 April 2020** (almost 4 years). Confirmed.

## Verified (kept)
- Resigned shadow Welsh sec (June 2016), returned ~4 months later to defence;
  defended Trident against Corbyn; moved to Wales brief 2020, out of shadow
  cabinet 2021. Confirmed.
- **Salisbury 2018**: broke with Corbyn — said Labour accepted Russia's
  responsibility, backed the security services / expulsion of 23 diplomats.
  Confirmed.
- Saudi-arms-suspension-pending-UN-Yemen stance; 2018 call for a defence
  industrial strategy; Trident survived into the **2017 manifesto**. Confirmed/
  consistent.
- **DSIS 2021**: published by the Conservative government after she left the
  defence brief; bio correctly says no evidence her 2018 push caused it. Confirmed.
- **Port Talbot Transition Board — £13.5m** released **15 Aug 2024**, **Jo
  Stevens** (Welsh Secretary) chaired/announced; board/fund predated the 2024
  election; Griffith a junior Wales minister attending. Confirmed exactly (bio's
  "collective, not her personal settlement" framing is right).
- **Conversion Practices draft Bill**: published **25 June 2026** under her
  successor; three new offences + protection orders; trans-inclusive; pre-
  legislative scrutiny; not yet law. Griffith (as equalities minister) had
  promised it; no draft when she left Sept 2025. Confirmed exactly.
- **Veterans presumption (2019)**: MoD consultation on presumption against
  prosecution after 10 years; she welcomed the proposals; Momentum/Labour-member
  backlash. Confirmed direction (bio notes it's political criticism, not
  misconduct).
