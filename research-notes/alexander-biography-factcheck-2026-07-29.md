## Verified against external sources
- **HS2** — exact: £46.8bn spent up to March 2026 (incl. £2.6bn on cancelled
  Phase 2); £153m on the current reset; 45 of 124 accepted review
  recommendations implemented (at Feb 2026, ~36%). NAO, June 2026.
- **ULEZ** — exact: City Hall assessment, roadside NO2 in inner London 21%
  lower than without the 2021 expansion (46% central); peer-reviewed by
  Imperial. Correctly flagged as Khan's policy, developed before she took the
  deputy-mayor job.
- **Luton Airport** — approved by Alexander 3 April 2025, against planning
  inspectors' recommendation to refuse. **Gatwick second runway** — authorised
  by her (announced Sept 2025). Both her decisions.
- **Nationalisation** — SWR (25 May 2025), c2c (20 July 2025), Greater Anglia
  (12 Oct 2025) under the Passenger Railway Services (Public Ownership) Act.
  The Act received royal assent 28 Nov 2024, the day before her 29 Nov 2024
  appointment — she implemented but did not originate it. Correct.
- **Driving-test backlog** — seven-week target moved to summer 2026, missed;
  average still ~22 weeks (now not expected until autumn 2027). Correct.
- Career: backed Burnham for leader, chaired Khan's mayoral campaign, backed
  Owen Smith vs Corbyn, first shadow cabinet minister to resign in the 2016
  revolt, "entirely dysfunctional" quote — all widely reported (Guardian).
