## Current role (per standing "always check the role is current" rule)
- **CONFIRMED current**: Burnham restored her as **Housing Secretary** (MHCLG)
  in the July 2026 reshuffle, replacing Steve Reed. She did **NOT** return as
  Deputy Prime Minister. The bio says only "restored her as Housing Secretary"
  (no DPM claim), so it is accurate. Sources: Property Week, Building, Today's
  Conveyancer, Mortgage Introducer.

## Verified against external sources (all exact — no trims required)
- **Housing delivery** (Full Fact): 208,600 homes in 2024/25, −6%, lowest since
  2015/16; 342,100 net additions 9 Jul 2024–15 Mar 2026 = ~22.8% of the 1.5m
  target; "off track". All exact.
- **£39bn Social and Affordable Homes Programme**: ~300,000 homes, 180,000 (60%)
  for social rent, runs 2026–2036 (ten years). Confirmed.
- **Planning, December 2024**: final NPPF (12 Dec 2024) restored mandatory
  targets, defined "grey belt" + golden rules, raised the annual target to
  370,000 (from ~300,000 standard-method). Confirmed. (July 2024 was the
  consultation; December 2024 was the final policy — bio's date is right.)
- **Renters' Rights Act**: Section 21 abolished 1 May 2026; advance rent capped
  at one month; fixed-term assured tenancies → rolling; rent rises once a year;
  became law after she resigned. Confirmed.
- **Employment Rights Act**: after Lords defeats the government U-turned — day-one
  unfair-dismissal promise dropped; qualifying period reduced to **six months**
  (from two years) from 1 Jan 2027. Bio's "two years to six months" is correct.
- **Stamp duty**: underpaid £40,000 (£30k paid vs £70k owed) on the £800,000
  East Sussex flat; standards adviser found integrity but a Ministerial Code
  breach (failed to follow written advice to get specialist tax guidance);
  resigned Sep 2025; HMRC later cleared her (no penalty, no deliberate/careless
  wrongdoing). Confirmed.
- 2020 leadership (backed Long-Bailey; separately elected deputy leader) and the
  May 2021 chair-removal episode: consistent with the record.
