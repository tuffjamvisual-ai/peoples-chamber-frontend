## Verified (kept)
- **Free home care** — Hammersmith & Fulham has not charged for home care since
  **1 April 2015**, the only English council to do so. Coleman later held the
  health/adult social care portfolio. Confirmed.
- Home-care model: **40 per cent increase in demand**, **minimum 30-minute
  visits**, **London Living Wage + travel time** for care workers. Confirmed.
- Disabled People's Commission (2016) and "Nothing About Disabled People
  Without Disabled People" (2018), co-production duties. Consistent (real H&F
  initiative).
- **Charing Cross** — 7-year campaign; demolition/A&E-downgrade plan abandoned
  2019 (Slaughter carried much); Labour's 2025 New Hospital Programme review put
  Charing Cross in the **third wave (build from 2035-2039)**. Confirmed;
  "preliminary work around 2030" consistent with pre-build.
- **Under-16 social-media ban** — **60+ Labour MPs** urged it; government
  announced its ban **15 June 2026**, adding livestreaming and contact-with-
  strangers restrictions; regulations to Parliament by end of year,
  implementation ~spring 2027 (not yet completed). Confirmed.
