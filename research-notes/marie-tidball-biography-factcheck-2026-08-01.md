## Verified (kept — confirmed)
- **2024 GE**: defeated **Miriam Cates** (Con), majority **8,739** (19.9%); Labour
  43.6%, Reform ~21.5%. Confirmed exactly. Congenital disability (four limbs) —
  confirmed; underpins the disability-rights framing.
- **Selection** backed by unions + South Yorkshire Labour (Dan Jarvis, Stephanie
  Peacock) — plausible/consistent, kept.
- **Welfare rebellion**: opposed the 2025 welfare bill (among the Labour rebels;
  37 at one reading, 47 at third reading). Confirmed she was a rebel.
- **PIP co-production amendment (NC11)**: called for the PIP review to be
  co-produced with disabled people, majority-disabled oversight, CRPD-aligned.
  **Stephen Timms called it a "helpful checklist" and accepted much of it**; she
  did NOT move it to a vote. Confirmed exactly.
- **Timms review steering group**: CONFIRMED REAL — announced 30 Oct 2025;
  co-chaired by **Sharon Brennan** and **Dr Clenton Farquharson CBE**; steering
  group majority disabled people / DPO reps; **12 members appointed 3 Feb 2026**;
  co-production began Feb 2026. (Scope/ME Association disputed how *genuine* the
  co-production was — but the group's existence + disabled co-chairs is confirmed,
  so the bio's factual claim stands. Bio is appropriately hedged.)
- **UC health-element cut**: the Act **halved** the health element for most NEW
  claimants **from 6 April 2026** (existing + severe-condition protected). **IFS:
  £5,079 → £2,556** (2025-26 terms) ≈ **£2,500/yr less**. Confirmed exactly.
- **Oxford community grants 2018-19**: **£1,460,970 (~£1.5m)**; Tidball was
  **Cabinet Member for Supporting Local Communities**. Confirmed. (The £4.3m
  leverage / 200,000 figures are attributed to council monitoring and the bio
  itself discredits the 200,000 — kept as self-caveated context.)
- **Assisted-dying bill**: she secured disability safeguards in the Commons
  version; bill fell when the session ended (consistent with the site's End of
  Life Bill canon). SEND £6,000 "notional budget" schools contribution is a real
  policy concept. Kept.
- DHP 2019-20 (85% of 602 applications) — minor, attributed to Oxford scrutiny
  committee, bio downplays her role. Kept.
