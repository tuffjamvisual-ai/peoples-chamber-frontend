## Verified (kept)
- Selection backing: Community, NUM, USDAW, SERA, CWU branches; Kinnock's steel
  endorsement. Consistent.
- **Steel Strategy 2026**: up to **half** of UK steel demand from domestic
  production; up to **£2.5bn** National Wealth Fund. Confirmed exactly.
- **Critical Minerals Strategy ("Vision 2035")**: **≥10%** domestic production,

  minister; **Steel Industry (Nationalisation) Bill May 2026** (Bill 4123);
  **Royal Assent 15 July 2026**; McDonald signed transfer regs, effect **16 July
  2026**. Confirmed exactly.

  furnaces; Network Rail supply. Confirmed.

- **NAO**: intervention not stabilising finances; **~£1.3m/day**, no fixed
  budget/repayment schedule; doubt British Steel can repay the "loan". Confirmed.
- **BCSSS**: **£2.3bn** reserve to **~40,000** members; a Budget 2025 (Reeves,
