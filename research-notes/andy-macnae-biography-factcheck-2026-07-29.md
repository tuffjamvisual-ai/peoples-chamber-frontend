  credited the IMF. Bio's "no evidence he personally secured it" holds.

  Fund secured 2013; five-year scheme 2014–2019; road layout reconfigured

  holder.
- **Haslingden Pool** — confirmed: MacNae portfolio holder for leisure and
  regeneration; council set aside/recommended £200k in July 2013; pool closed
  2013; no replacement delivered.
