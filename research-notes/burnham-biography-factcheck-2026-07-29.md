# Internal research note — Andy Burnham biography fact-check (2026-07-29)

Internal only. NOT for publication with the bio. Backs the biography now on

## Verified against external sources
- Rough sleeping **268 (2017) → 89 (2021) → 197 (2025)**, risen four consecutive
  years — matches sources exactly. "End rough sleeping by 2020" pledge and the
  2019 admission he'd miss it: confirmed. A Bed Every Night + national Everyone In
  (pandemic): confirmed.
- Bee Network — first area outside London to franchise buses, ending ~38 years of
  1980s deregulation, **"delivered on time and on budget"** (source phrasing).
  Operator opposition / legal challenge: real.
- Housing — 2024 pledge of **10,000 council homes by 2028** and the push to
  **suspend Right to Buy**: confirmed. GM lost ~24,000 homes to RTB over 20 years.
- Career — Gordon Brown's cabinet, **two Labour leadership runs (2010, 2015)**,
  left Parliament 2017 for the mayoralty, **October 2020 tier-restrictions
  standoff**: all correct.

Sources: The Conversation (Burnham 10-year record); The Mill (rough sleeping up a
4th year); GMCA (Bee Network); Social Housing (10,000 homes + RTB suspension).
