# Internal research note — Alex Mayer biography fact-check (2026-07-29)

Internal only. Backs the biography on her profile (`mp_biography.political_bio`,
member_id 5141, Dunstable and Leighton Buzzard). Backbencher. Notably one of the

## Verified (kept)
- **MEP succession**: took the East of England MEP seat in 2016 when Richard
  Howitt resigned (closed-list, no election; she was his former press
  secretary); lost 2019; won Dunstable and Leighton Buzzard 2024. Confirmed.
- **Amazon "Printed in Dunstable"**: after Mayer's suggestion (a story about a
  book that didn't know where it was printed), Amazon added the credit to
  paperbacks from its Dunstable print-on-demand facility (~200 staff, up to
  120,000 books/day). Confirmed.
- **"Churchill Time"** (Double British Summer Time): Mayer pressed for it;
  Business Minister Justin Madders rejected it (27 March 2025), citing the
  NI/Ireland "time border" and daylight-use concerns. Confirmed.
- **Bus network inquiry**: Transport Committee "Buses connecting communities"
  report recommended an under-22s free-travel pilot; government rejected it as
  "unaffordable" given the Spending Review. Confirmed.
- **Cruelty-free Commons campaign**: real; by 31 March 2025 use of further
  non-cruelty-free products had ceased. Confirmed in outline. CFI 8m-signature
  UN petition (2018) is a real CFI event; her involvement consistent with her
  confirmed CFI work.
