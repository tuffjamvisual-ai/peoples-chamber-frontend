## CORRECTION — "Quad Bikes Bill became an Act" was FALSE, fixed
- Provided bio claimed it was a "clearer legislative achievement", that she "secured its
  passage through Parliament", produced "resulting legislation" / "the Act", and "delivered
  the legal framework"; capability section said she "converted one narrower campaign into
  legislation" and her "greatest completed policy achievement was a focused regulatory Act".
- **All false.** The Quad Bikes Bill (bills.parliament.uk bill 3136) was a **Ten Minute
  Rule Bill** introduced **15 Mar 2022**, listed for 2nd reading (6 May) but made **no
  further progress** and **fell when the 2021-22 session prorogued**. Never became law;
  no Act; no registration/immobiliser framework enacted. Verified two ways (bill page +
  Commons Library CDP-2022-0097).
- Action: rewrote the two "delivery" paragraphs to state it did not become law (introduced
  under ten-minute rule, proposed registration/immobilisers, fell at prorogation — a
  campaigning measure, not a completed reform); changed "converted ... into legislation" ->
  "pressed a sustained campaign on quad-bike regulation"; replaced "greatest completed
  policy achievement was a focused regulatory Act" -> "She has no completed Act of her own:
  her quad-bikes bill fell without progressing"; softened the closing "practical result" ->
  "practical, if temporary, gains" (referring to the dental pilot). Nothing invented.
  Flagged to user.
