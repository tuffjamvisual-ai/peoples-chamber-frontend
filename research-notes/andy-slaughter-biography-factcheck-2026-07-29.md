## Current role (per standing "always check the role is current" rule)
- **CONFIRMED**: **Solicitor General, appointed by Andy Burnham on 21 July
  2026** (Law Officers' Department, under Attorney General Ellie Reeves; does
  not attend Cabinet). Previously chaired the Justice Committee (elected Sept
  2024). MP for Hammersmith and Chiswick. Barrister. The Law Officers' remit
  (CPS, SFO, Government Legal Department) is accurate.

## Verified (kept)
- **Heathrow 2009** — resigned a government position (PPS) on 27 January 2009
  to oppose the third runway. Confirmed. (Rebel count / majority not confirmed
  — see trims.)
- June 2016 frontbench resignation during the anti-Corbyn revolt; returned as
  housing spokesman; sacked June 2017 over the single-market/customs-union
  rebellion (~50 Labour rebels — consistent with the Umunna Queen's Speech
  amendment). Kept.
- Shadow solicitor general under Starmer; left Nov 2023 over the Gaza ceasefire
  vote (~10 frontbenchers). Consistent with record. Kept.
- **Charing Cross Hospital** — 2013 "Shaping a Healthier Future" plans to
  downgrade A&E; **Matt Hancock abandoned the programme, 26 March 2019**;
  campaign led by residents, Save Our Hospitals H&F and the council with
  Slaughter. Confirmed.
- **CCRC / Karen Kneller** — Justice Committee (Slaughter chair) found her
  position untenable (May 2025) after the Malkinson case (Chris Henley KC
  review: he could have been freed 10 years earlier); **she resigned in July
  2025.** Confirmed.
- **2010 standards** — Commissioner John Lyon; misuse of publicly funded
  stationery and prepaid envelopes for a housing-meeting invitation that became
  party-political; **accepted, apologised, repaid £15.95**, complaint closed.
  Confirmed (resolution letter re: 13 Jan 2010 meeting).
- **Hamas meetings 2010 controversy** — revived on his SG appointment
  (JNS / others: "Hamas row"); met Hamas-linked figures in 2010, argued for
  engagement; at the time only the military wing was proscribed; **whole
  organisation proscribed 2021**; meetings not found unlawful. Confirmed.
- LASPO 2012 (Slaughter led Labour opposition); FOI-extension bill for private
  contractors (did not become law). Kept.
