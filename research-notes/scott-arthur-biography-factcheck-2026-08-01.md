## Verified (kept — confirmed)
- **2024 GE**: defeated SNP's **Joanna Cherry**; **40.9%**, majority **6,217**
  (13.6%). Confirmed exactly. Edinburgh councillor (2017) + transport & environment
  convener (2022, Labour minority admin). Confirmed.
- **Rare Cancers Act**: his **Private Member's Bill** (introduced 16 Oct 2024),
  **Royal Assent 5 March 2026**. Rare cancer = affects **no more than 1 in 2,000**
  (the bio's definition is the correct one; a search summary saying "more than"
  was garbled). Duties on research/treatment/clinical trials + NHS data to find
  trial participants + a named government lead. Confirmed. (PMBs rarely pass — the
  "<5% 2010-24" framing is sound; "sixth in the ballot" plausible, kept.)
- **LEZ**: enforcement began **June 2024**; **>95% compliant** after year one;
  **penalty notices fell 56% June 2024 to January 2025**. Confirmed exactly. Modal
  shift to PT/active travel — plausible, kept; bio's "doesn't yet prove air-quality
  gains" caveat accurate.
- **Council fleet failure**: **41 non-compliant** council vehicles despite a
  two-year grace period; Arthur's categorical pledge ("every single one of the
  council vehicles will be LEZ compliant") — CONFIRMED and broken; **~£2,000/day**
  for rentals; **£28,770** an accurate INITIAL figure. NOTE: total later rose to
  ~£302,000 — the bio's "initial £28,770" is accurate but understates the eventual
  bill (left as the user framed it). "38 replacement vehicles" not separately
  confirmed but consistent; kept.
- **Pavement parking**: first Scottish city, enforcement **29 Jan 2024**, **£100**
  penalty (£50 if paid in 14 days), **5,000+ roads** examined; Arthur quoted as
  convener; legal power from the **Transport (Scotland) Act**. Confirmed exactly.
- **Fireworks**: after 2024 Bonfire Night "mayhem" he wrote to supermarkets; all
  major supermarkets in his seat (Tesco, Morrisons, Sainsbury's, Lidl, Aldi) agreed
  to stop selling; later pushed for wider controls (June 2026). Confirmed.
- North-south tram extension still at consultation/financing — plausible, kept.
