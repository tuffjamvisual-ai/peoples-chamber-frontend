## Verified (kept)
- **By-election**: Scott Benton (Con) caught in a newspaper lobbying sting
  (suspended 27 Feb 2024, resigned 25 Mar 2024); Webb won **2 May 2024** on a
  **26-point swing** (26.3). Turnout 32.5% (typical low by-election turnout;
  kept). GE two months later retained; Reform ~29%. Confirmed.
- **Welfare rebellion**: March 2025 first 2024-intake Labour MP to condemn the
  disability-benefit cuts ("devastating"/"not in the manifesto", Sky News);
  voted against the **UC & PIP Bill**; **among 49 Labour MPs against at SECOND
  reading (1 July 2025, 335-260)**. Confirmed exactly. NB: distinct from the
  Hinchliff bio's "47 at third reading (9 July)" — different votes, both correct.
- **Infant formula**: DoH clarified food banks may supply formula in
  emergencies; **almost 3 million individual feeds** provided via food
  banks/community groups; Feed charity + Metro + 100k+ petition; **December 2025**
  PM reform allowing **loyalty points/vouchers** for formula + clearer info;
  **up to £500/year** saving (CMA range £300-£500 per child's first year).
  Confirmed exactly. CMA had done the market study/recommendations. Confirmed.
- **Two-child limit**: removed from **Universal Credit from 6 April 2026**
  (UC (Removal of Two Child Limit) Act 2026); est. **450,000 children** lifted
  from poverty. Confirmed exactly. Framed as a shared campaign — kept.
- **VAT**: his call for a permanent **12.5%** tourism/hospitality rate not
  adopted; government instead cut VAT to **5%** on children's meals, tickets and
  family attractions **25 June-1 Sept 2026**; accommodation/most hospitality at
  standard rate. Confirmed (bio's "June-September 2026" is accurate).
- **Bathing water**: Blackpool South/Central "sufficient", Blackpool North
  "poor" — his own acknowledgment, kept.
- Neighbourhood/Pride-in-Place £20m-over-10-years award; road allocation by
  formula — kept as illustrations of the "bundling" critique.

  million"**. Webb's actual/current claim (Blackpool Gazette, two-years-in-office)
  is "record levels of investment... more than £150 million in two years". The
  £120m figure could not be confirmed; £150m+ is the confirmed claim. The bio's
  critique (the total bundles disparate departmental/council/national funding;
  "evidence does not show Webb personally secured the whole amount") is unchanged
