# Internal research note — Dame Angela Eagle biography fact-check (2026-07-29)

Internal only. NOT for publication. Backs the biography on Eagle's profile
(`mp_biography.political_bio`, member_id 491, Wallasey). Supplied pre-sourced
(inline citation tags); tags stripped before publication, as with every prior
cabinet bio.

## Verified against external sources
- **Asylum backlog** — exact: cases awaiting an initial decision fell 18% to
  **70,532** (covering 90,812 people) at end June 2025. Home Office / Migration
  Observatory.
- **Asylum claims** — exact: **111,084** in the year ending June 2025, +14%,
  the highest since records began (1979). Home Office.
- **Came out publicly in 1997** — September 1997, in The Observer; first openly
  lesbian government minister; MP for Wallasey since 1992.
- **Defra Secretary from 20 July 2026** (Burnham reshuffle, succeeding Emma
  Reynolds) — confirmed by farming trade press (Farmers Weekly, FarmingUK,
  Agriland, Circular Online, letsrecycle, Water Magazine, Meat Management).
  **£290m Sustainable Farming Incentive** (SFI26) and the **Farming and Food
  Partnership Board** (met twice, commissioned poultry/horticulture Sector
  Growth Plans) — both real Defra 2026 items. She was formerly Minister of
  State for food security and rural affairs, so "returns to Defra" is correct.
- Border Security, Asylum and Immigration Bill / Act 2025, Border Security
  Command on a statutory footing, became law after she left the brief
  (she was Minister for Border Security and Asylum 8 Jul 2024 – 6 Sep 2025) —
  correct.

## Trimmed before publication (Burnham rule — could not pin down this session)
- **Returns "10,652 … 31 per cent more"** (year to June 2025) → dropped the
  exact figure; kept "Returns also increased over the same period." Search only
  confirmed a different-period figure (6,781, +23%, Jul 2024–Mar 2025).
- **Hotels "rose by 8 per cent to 32,059"** → dropped; one source gives ~36,000
  in hotels for the year ending June 2025, so the exact figure conflicts. Kept
  "The number … rose, despite the government promising to end hotel use."
- **"About 43,600 … by small boat"** and **small-boat returns "fell by 7 per
  cent to 2,330"** → softened to "Tens of thousands … by small boat" and
  "returns of people who had arrived by small boat fell." Qualitative points
  kept.
- **Leadership challenge "Owen Smith secured 90 nominations … against her 72"**
  → dropped the exact split (searches returned conflicting counts); kept "after
  it became clear that Owen Smith had secured more nominations … than she had."
- **"a party investigation found that she received hundreds of … messages"** →
  softened to "she received abusive and homophobic messages" (the investigation
  detail and the "hundreds" count could not be confirmed; the abuse itself is
  well documented).
- **NFU "described its dealings with her as constructive and straight-talking"**
  → generalised to "Farming leaders described their early dealings with her as
  constructive" (specific NFU quote not verified this session).
- Countryside Stewardship extension: kept, but dropped the unverified "for a
  year" duration.
