# LGSCO annual review datasheets

Local Government and Social Care Ombudsman, annual review of local government complaints, datasheets for 2024-25 and
2025-26: complaints received, complaints decided, remedy and compliance outcomes. Kept here unchanged so
`scripts/import-lgsco.js` can rebuild `council_ombudsman`.

- Listed at https://www.lgo.org.uk/information-centre/reports/annual-review-reports/local-government-complaint-reviews
- Downloaded by the site owner in a browser on 20 Oct 2026 (the files sit under `/assets/attach/`, which lgo.org.uk's
  robots.txt disallows for crawlers, so they are not fetched by script).
- How to read them: https://www.lgo.org.uk/information-centre/reports/annual-review-reports/interpreting-local-authority-statistics
